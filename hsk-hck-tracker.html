HSK/HCK Ops Tracker — Built with Cowork AI

Designing and shipping a live reporting tool, not just the mockup for one

Every week, I need to report progress on KPI compliance, HSE observations, tasks, and manpower status up to my manager at ADNOC Refining — and every week, that meant digging through scattered spreadsheets and rebuilding the same summary by hand. So I designed and built the HSK/HCK KPI, Tasks & Observations Tracker: a live internal dashboard that pulls all of it into one place, in a format built for how a manager actually reads a status update.



What makes this one different from the rest of my design work: I didn't just design it, I shipped it — using Cowork, Anthropic's AI-powered work app, as my build partner. I directed the design decisions (layout, hierarchy, what a manager needs to see first versus what belongs one click deeper) and Cowork handled turning that direction into a working tool. It's a good example of where my design thinking goes beyond Figma files: knowing what a good operational tool looks like, and knowing how to actually get one built and into daily use.



A quick note on what you're seeing below: these two screens are wireframes recreating the tool's layout for this portfolio. The live tracker is connected to real, continuously-updating company data, which I can't share publicly — so what's here shows the structure and design thinking, not the actual production dashboard.


Dashboard view

The landing view is built for a five-second read: KPI compliance as a single progress ring, HSE observations status, open tasks split by in-progress vs. on hold, and manpower numbers — total headcount, on duty today, and vacant positions — all visible without scrolling or clicking into anything.



Details view

One tab over, the same data opens up into the specifics — who's pending which course, the exact observation logged and its clarification status, individual task ownership, and open vacancy details by team and position code. Same information architecture as the dashboard, just one layer deeper for whoever needs to act on it.
