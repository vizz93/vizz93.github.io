My Design Approach

Brief & Requirement Intake

Every project starts with meeting end users to define design needs, deliverable specs, and deadlines before any design work begins. Clear scope up front prevents rework later.

Asset & Reference Collection

Pulling from an organized media library — past jobs, stock imagery, brand guidelines — keeps every new piece visually consistent with what's come before, and speeds up production.

Concept & Layout Development

Applying grid, typography, and brand identity principles to translate the brief into a first layout, balancing creative direction with practical production constraints.

Stakeholder Review & Proofing

Producing proof copies for client approval and for printing technicians to use as a production reference, ensuring everyone signs off before work moves downstream.

Pre-Press Quality Control

Checking the design against equipment and paper stock requirements before it goes to print, catching issues early rather than after a costly reprint.

Production, Handover & Confidentiality

Finalizing and handing off the asset, while keeping any sensitive or unreleased material properly restricted throughout the process.
