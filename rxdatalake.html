RXDataLake — Centralized Reporting Architecture

Presenting how data flows from every touchpoint into one system that powers RedeemX reporting

View on Figma

RedeemX pulls data from a lot of different places — kiosks, mobile apps, venue systems, and more — and all of it eventually needs to land somewhere useful: a single, reliable source that powers reporting across the whole platform. This presentation walks through exactly that: how raw data from scattered points gets funneled into a centralized data lake, and how that centralized system becomes the foundation for every report generated downstream.



The goal was to make a fairly technical data-architecture concept easy for both technical and non-technical stakeholders to follow — visualizing the journey from source to storage to report, rather than just describing it in the abstract.
